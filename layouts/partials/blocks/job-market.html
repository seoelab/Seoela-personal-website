Environmental Regulation, Firms, and Trade:
Evidence from Mexico’s Air-Quality Programs

How does local environmental regulation affect firms and welfare? I study Mexico’s ProAire programs using linked firm-level data and a quantitative spatial model that accounts for trade, pollution transport, and worker sorting.

Teaching

Indiana University Bloomington · Boise State University

I have served as the primary instructor for an international economics course as well as a teaching assistant in microeconomics, advanced microeconomic theory, and behavioral economics.

I strive to teach students how to think like economists by introducing a basic process to analyze public policy questions. In particular, I guide students to:

  1. Articulatethe economic question
  2. Understandthe mechanisms
  3. Examinethe evidence
  4. Discusspolicy implications
